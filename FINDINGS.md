# Findings & Recommendations

## Scope

This analysis focused on three of the five coupon types (Bar, Coffee House, and Restaurant(<$20)) chosen to compare a habit-driven/socially-sensitive coupon (Bar), a moderately habit- and time-sensitive coupon (Coffee House), and a broadly popular, low-commitment coupon (Restaurant<20). Carry Out & Take Away and Restaurant(20-50) were not analyzed in depth here; the same methodology (habit-frequency splits, time-of-day, passenger type, and demographic breakdowns) could be extended to these types in future work.

## Bar Coupons

**Finding:** Acceptance is driven primarily by existing habit. Passenger context matters nearly as much. Age has a smaller secondary effect.

**Recommendation:** Target bar coupon delivery to drivers with a known or inferred history of frequent bar visits, and suppress delivery when a child passenger is present. That scenario converts at less than half the rate of a no-kid, frequent-visitor scenario.

## Coffee House Coupons

**Finding:** Habit matters, but less than for bars. Time of day is a stronger factor. Drivers under 21 accept notably more often than any other age group. Driving alone has the lowest acceptance; driving with friends has the highest, with no penalty for a child passenger.

**Recommendation:** Concentrate coffee coupon delivery in the mid-morning window (around 10AM) rather than distributing it evenly across the day. Consider slightly higher delivery volume or lower thresholds for younger drivers (under 21), who show unusually high responsiveness.

## Restaurant (<$20) Coupons

**Finding:** Acceptance is high and broad-based, largely unaffected by habit or income. Timing and passenger company matter more. Company raises acceptance with no penalty for a child passenger.

**Recommendation:** This coupon type can be sent with less demographic/habit targeting than bar or coffee coupons. It converts well across most driver segments. The main lever is timing. Concentrate delivery around meal hours (midday and early evening) to maximize conversion.

## Weather / Temperature (Cross-Coupon)

**Finding:** Acceptance is modestly higher at 80°F than at 30°F or 55°F, a smaller effect than habit or passenger type but consistent across large samples at each temperature.

**Recommendation:** Weather can be used as a minor secondary signal, but shouldn't be prioritized over habit, timing, or passenger context, which show much larger effects on acceptance.

## Summary

The strength of each predictive factor varies by coupon type. Bar coupons should be targeted narrowly (habit + no children present). Coffee house coupons benefit most from time-of-day targeting. Restaurant (<$20) coupons are the most universally appealing and require the least targeting, though timing still helps. Across all types, passenger context and timing are more actionable, real-time signals than static demographics like income.
